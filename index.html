I am a Master's student in Computer Science (Data Science, Analytics and AI) at Carleton University, where I am a member of the Alternative Learning Technologies and Games (ALT Games) Lab, advised by Prof. Edward Melcer. I received my B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning from Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering (University of Mumbai), graduating with a CGPA of 9.04/10.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and games. My previous research was in computer vision for remote sensing: detecting and tracking maritime vessels across SAR and optical satellite imagery, and reconstructing lunar terrain from orbital images. I have also worked on deep learning for pediatric speech assessment. Alongside research, I have spent years building games, game engines and game AI, and I run an indie game development studio, Bluefyre Studio.

Research interests: AI for games, human–computer interaction, computer vision, remote sensing and geospatial AI, applied deep learning.

Research Projects

  • Kitagawa: Multi-Sensor Maritime Domain Awareness Sep 2025 – Jul 2026
    B.Tech. final year project; NCIIPC–DPIIT–FITT AI Grand Challenge (top 14 nationally)
    • Docker-based, GPU-accelerated inference pipeline using YOLOv11n for ship detection on Sentinel-1 SAR (trained on OpenSARShip v2) and Sentinel-2 electro-optical imagery.
    • Post-processing with non-maximum suppression, land masking against GSHHG shoreline polygons, and ConvNeXt-based cloud filtering for optical scenes.
    • Detection timestamping from SAR calibration vectors and EO granule metadata; correlation of detections with interpolated AIS vessel tracks for trajectory analysis.
    • Geospatial processing with Rasterio, GDAL and GeoPandas; Prefect-based workflow orchestration for scalable, reproducible deployment. Deployed end-to-end on NTRO servers at IIT Delhi.
    • Quantitative results are not public, as the work was carried out for a national defence challenge.
    [Code] [Project page] [Credential]
  • High-Resolution Lunar DEMs via Photoclinometry Jun 2025 – Aug 2025
    ISRO Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon 2025, grand finalist (top 9 nationally)
    • Developed and presented a novel approach for generating high-resolution lunar digital elevation models using photoclinometry (shape-from-shading).
    • Presented at the Indian Space Research Organisation's National Remote Sensing Centre, Hyderabad. Worked with ISSDC lunar data products.
    [Event]
  • AutoDEAP: Pediatric Speech Misarticulation Detection Oct 2024 – Aug 2025
    • A hybrid CNN–Transformer model that fuses MFCC and Mel-spectrogram audio features with speaker metadata to detect speech sound errors in children aged 5–10, supporting early and remote screening by speech-language pathologists.
    • Trained on 7,381 annotated samples; reached 72.90% accuracy and a 71.36% F1 score, outperforming ResNet (63.48%) and plain CNN (71.33%) baselines.
    [Paper] [Code] [Web app]
